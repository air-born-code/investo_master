- **No W32 prices, market caps or financial metrics.** `weekly_metrics.csv` holds only 2026-07-18 rows. Every valuation reference in this issue is three weeks old and labelled as such. We cannot assess expected return, asymmetry or whether the cycle signal has been priced.
- **No W32 scores.** `scores.csv` ends at 2026-W29. Rankings and confidence levels in this issue are carried forward, not re-derived.
- **Eighty-one filings are collected but unread.** This includes 25 quarterly reports for the period ending 2026-06-30 and every filing for GEV, VRT and MP since the W29 baseline. This is the largest gap in the store by a wide margin.
- **No July CPI.** The inflation rows in the W32 macro set carry a June observation date. Any statement about the inflation trajectory into July would be invention.
- **Payroll history is two observations deep.** June and July only. Insufficient to characterise a trend.
- **The store holds no scenario data for any asset.** There is no failure/bear/base/bull/generational structure recorded for gev, vrt, crdo, rklb or mp. §7 was omitted for that reason.
- **Every gate for every candidate is `not_assessed`.** The ledger contains no evidence references and no assessment dates.
- **Universe source history is incomplete in this working set.** 231 older source rows for universe-tier assets were withheld. Statements about which universe assets have or have not filed are statements about the visible rows only.
- **Filing metadata does not describe contents.** Where a form type implies something (a 425 implies merger communication), I have said so and stopped there. Counterparties, terms and consideration for the NEE/D, ETN and MOD filings are unknown from the store.
- **No W31 collection appears to have occurred.** The batch accessed 2026-08-08 covers filings from 2026-07-27, spanning two weeks. Whether anything between collections was missed cannot be determined from the store.
- **Forty-three universe-tier assets carry `last_reviewed_date` blank** and notes reading "Web-sourced lead, unverified thesis." Their theme mappings are screen assignments, not researched conclusions.
